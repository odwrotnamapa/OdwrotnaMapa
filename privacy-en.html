Privacy Policy — Odwrotna Mapa

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Odwrotna Mapa is a free, open-source map application. It does not require an account, contains no ads or analytics/tracking tools, and does not sell or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data stored locally on your device

The following data stays exclusively on your device and is never sent to the app's author:

  • Saved favorite places and routes
  • Your selected interface language
  • Data cached for offline use (e.g. Wikipedia summaries for favorite places), so the app can work without an internet connection

Third-party services used by the app

For the app to display the map, search for places, and show additional information, some data (e.g. your search query or the coordinates of a point you tapped on the map) is sent to the following independent services. None of them are controlled by the author of Odwrotna Mapa — each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — place search, map tile rendering, and reverse geocoding (turning a tap on the map into a place name)
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia — article previews for selected places
  • Mapillary (a Meta-owned service) — optional street view. This feature is entirely optional — the app works normally without using it

App permissions

The app may ask for access to your location solely to show it on the map — it is never stored or transmitted beyond the requests to OpenStreetMap described above, which only occur when you actively use the search or place-information features.

Source code

Odwrotna Mapa is fully open source — you can independently verify the above by reviewing the code on GitHub.
